## Findings requiring action

1. **Thirteen facts need applicability conditions before general comparison use.** Their underlying values are generally supported, but their current unqualified form can imply range-wide equipment. Preserve existing quotes and add the exact supporting table/footnote provenance when editing.

   - **Brezza C1-002, C1-003, C1-004, C1-005, C1-006:** scope the 64-colour ambient lighting, ventilated seats, 10.1-inch display, 360 camera and app-store feature family to ZXi+. The current official brochure page 10 marks these in ZXi+; other columns differ or are unavailable. They are usable for the chosen ZXi+ candidate after qualification, not as universal Brezza equipment.
   - **Brezza C1-009:** front sensors are ZXi Turbo / ZXi+ equipment; rear sensors are wider-ranging. The combined front-and-rear claim needs those conditions or should be scoped to selected ZXi+.
   - **Brezza C1-010:** active-cooled wireless charging is listed for ZXi and ZXi+. Add that trim scope.
   - **Venue C2-003:** add R16 wheels plus roof rails to the 1,665 mm height. C4-003 already preserves this condition; C4-004 correctly records the 1,650 mm R15 alternative. Prefer those qualified facts over the generic duplicate.
   - **Venue C2-011, C2-014, C2-015:** SmartSense ADAS, Bose eight-speaker audio and 12.3-inch navigation are HX10 / HX10 Knight features. Confirmed by official feature tables 2, rows 33–48; and 5, rows 2 and 6. They apply to the selected regular HX10, but not the whole Venue range.
   - **Venue C2-016:** diamond-cut R16 wheels are HX7, HX8 and regular HX10 in official table 3, row 4. Knight columns have a separate wheel treatment; do not broaden the claim.
   - **Venue C4-019:** the three-year Bluelink term lacks equipped-trim applicability and the paid-renewal condition. Table 5, rows 12–13 identifies the equipped variants; brochure page 21 says paid service follows the three-year period. The selected HX10 is equipped. Avoid suggesting every Venue includes perpetual free connectivity.

   Sources: [Brezza brochure, page 10](https://www.marutisuzuki.com/content/dam/msil/arena/in/en/assets/cars/brezza/brochures/new_brezza_brochure.pdf), [Venue feature tables](https://www.hyundai.com/in/en/find-a-car/venue/features), [Venue brochure, pages 18 and 21](https://www.hyundai.com/content/dam/hyundai/in/en/data/brochure/venue.pdf).

Do not infer a practical boot-space winner by comparing Venue's VDA-labelled figure with another maker's differently labelled measurement without establishing comparability.
