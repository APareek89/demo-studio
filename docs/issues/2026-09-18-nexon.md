## Issues

### NX01 — Follow-up question discarded as agreement
Where: player check-in. Steps: type `Yes, but what about charging?`. Expected: answer the question. Actual: `Good — moving on.` and next slide. Severity: wrong for the customer. Cause: short prefix-matching treats any short reply starting with yes as confirmation. Status: fixed; browser regression passes. Test: exact confirmations settle choices; qualified replies reach Q&A intact.

### NX02 — The guide resumes behind a callback form
Where: unknown-answer path. Steps: ask exact on-road price in Mumbai. Expected: explain the gap and retain the conversational turn. Actual: callback form stays open while narration and another check-in continue. Severity: wrong for the customer. Cause: unknown and dissatisfied paths immediately call resumeAfterQA. Status: fixed; browser regression passes. Test: explicit Continue is required; dismissing a form does not advance narration.

### NX03 — Questions can advance without an answer
Where: normal narration, opening, custom batches and check-ins. Expected: every real question creates a response turn. Actual: ordinary narration has no question boundary; check-ins show an eight-second continuation countdown. Severity: wrong for the customer. Cause: content/flow mismatch and timer-driven turn completion. Status: countdown observed in browser; narration mismatch confirmed in code and iQube script. Test: silence and transcription never resolve a customer question; explicit Skip/Continue does.

### NX04 — A late transcript can resolve the wrong turn
Where: speech capture and waitFor. Expected: a transcript belongs to the question that started recording. Actual: callbacks test only whether any waiter exists; the countdown can expire between recording and transcription. Severity: wrong for the customer. Cause: no turn ownership. Status: fixed; synthetic late-speech cases after skip/restart pass. Real microphone quality remains untested.

### NX05 — Clarification and opening interruption lose continuity
Where: askAndListen and resumeAfterQA. Expected: voice or typed clarification, then resume the saved point after follow-ups. Actual: clarification is microphone-only; an opening interruption can restart the opening because the route has not been built. Severity: wrong for the customer. Status: fixed; typed clarification and opening-return browser regressions pass.

### NX06 — Product is obscured at the first impression
Where: player launch/intake. Expected: product-led presentation with a short, useful invitation. Actual: large mascot, blurred product and generic centred text. Long iQube takeaway fills the opening. Severity: wrong for the customer. Status: redesigned and inspected on desktop/phone with mock content; final Nexon art direction awaits its permitted source pack.

### NX07 — Slides lack hierarchy and callout lines can point from the wrong place
Where: slide renderer. Expected: a visible benefit title, appropriate product image, readable evidence, accurate annotation geometry. Actual: repetitive panel presentation; title primarily lives in progress buttons; a revealed overlay's line starts at the top-left rather than its chip. Severity: wrong for the customer. Cause: layout computed while callout hidden; no recompute after reveal. Status: fixed; reveal geometry and native-aspect wide/narrow browser regressions pass.

### NX08 — Fallback labels are unfinished fragments
Where: derived deck. Expected: short complete labels or omission when no safe concise text exists. Actual iQube: `Charger supplied: portable new 950W charger that plugs`, clipped titles and disclaimers. Severity: wrong for the customer. Cause: blind word slicing. Status: fallback fixed and covered by content regressions; new real Nexon deck still needs review.

### NX09 — Script ignores stated customer context
Where: real iQube opening. Expected: use the customer's 5–10 km commute and low-cost priority. Actual: repeated 30 km/day examples, repeated discovery and long specification lists. Severity: wrong for the customer. Cause: old script reuse, unavailable runtime personalisation, conflicting schema/prompt wording and unlabelled assumptions. Status: confirmed in session; align prompts and explicit interaction metadata, then verify new real script against actual customer profiles.

### NX10 — Input availability and state are unclear
Where: intake. Expected: visible typing alternative and accurate listening/transcribing state. Actual mock browser: `Listening — just talk` without a visible typing field; the top status remains Ready, requiring the conversation drawer to type. Severity: wrong for the customer. Status: observed in browser; browser microphone support is not proof of real STT failure. Test: keyboard input is always available and stalled/denied mic does not strand the turn.

### NX11 — The customer sees internal sales scoring at the end
Where: Stop → handoff on the revised product browser. Steps: Stop during an unknown-answer hold. Expected: a useful recap of explored topics, open questions and an optional next step. Actual: purchase-intent score31/100, customer-state and personalization flags, route/drop point and USP ids; the callback form can remain above the summary. Severity: wrong for the customer. Cause: the internal dealership/session view doubles as the customer ending. Status: fixed; final buyer-recap and overlay browser regression passes. Internal session record remains intact; buyer recap uses actual conversation data.
