### NX19 — First Build fails at voice and mixes recorded speakers
Where: first real Studio Build, voice stage; `dm_36d47b86`, commit `a5a663b`. Steps: complete the reviewed Align edits, approve all six cards and click Build. Expected: all required narration, FAQ and filler clips in the locked Sarvam/Priya voice, then a ready bundle. Actual: the voice stage failed after 128.5 seconds with “Voice bank incomplete: 11 FAQ answer(s) and 14 filler line(s) have no audio.” No bundle was created. Severity: blocks the demo.

Exact file audit: 60/85 required clips exist. All 18 main lines, 18 deeper lines, 7 check-ins, 2 closing lines and 1 intake have audio. Missing FAQ IDs are Q05, Q10, Q11, Q12, Q13, Q14, Q16, Q17, Q18, Q19 and Q20; 14 filler IDs are listed in the integrity report. Exact provider/speaker/language/text cache hashes identify 50 Sarvam/Priya references and 10 Gemini/Sulafat references. Cause: three observed Sarvam 429 responses tripped the provider circuit, and the build-time fallback chain switched to Gemini despite the selected voice lock. The cache lookup happened after the circuit check, so even an existing selected-voice clip could be skipped. FAQ/filler reuse checked only whether an audio path existed, allowing a second-voice cached path to survive a retry. Gemini calls also produced input-block refusals and a closed-client failure. Recommendation: enforce the selected provider/speaker/language throughout main, FAQ, filler and runtime paths; reuse its valid content-hash cache during a cooldown; invalidate mismatched references and retry missing selected-voice audio after bounded rate-limit handling. Do not silently switch voices. Status: selected-voice/cache/rate-limit fix implemented; 27/27 focused contracts pass with fake transports and outbound sockets blocked.
