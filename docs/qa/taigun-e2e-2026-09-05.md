## Findings closed in this gate

- **P1 — crash-test/test-drive intent collision:** a hyphenated crash-test question selected the test-drive fact. Fixed with a hard safety-rating unknown and regression coverage.
- **P1 — mobile horizontal overflow:** nested grid tracks and the closed conversation panel widened the document. Fixed with shrinkable tracks, wrapped controls and live scroll-width assertions.
- **P1 — human corrections could be regenerated over:** direct edits marked their own stage stale. Fixed with validated stage completion plus downstream invalidation and re-approval.
- **P1 — non-visual claims used attractive but irrelevant images:** price, capacity, terms and hidden safety claims could inherit generic product imagery. Fixed by gating observability before visual similarity.
- **P1 — provider exhaustion made long stages fragile:** FAQ retries restarted work and repeated failed calls. Fixed with provider failover, per-answer checkpoints, registry-hash reuse, explicit retry and runtime cooldown.
- **P2 — comparison and finance fallback shape:** named rival answers omitted Taigun; named EMI scenarios could return siblings. Fixed with contract-specific answer shapes and mandatory caveats.
- **P2 — voice review drift:** displayed settings could differ from the resolved voice, and a new sample did not invalidate approval. Fixed and verified.
- **P2 — stale workflow and upload copy:** five-card build guidance and hidden CSV support corrected and covered by sync tests.
