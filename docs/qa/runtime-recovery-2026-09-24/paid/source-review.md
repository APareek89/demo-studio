# Independent source checklist — CRETA final QA

Source boundary: only `/Users/macbook/Documents/Hyundai Creta Official/Demo files/Hyundai_CRETA_Complete_Guide.pdf` and the 16 JPGs beside it. No websites, previous demos, provider calls or protected data were consulted. The PDF has 49 pages, dated 19 September 2026; p49 explicitly calls it a compilation, **not a Hyundai publication**. Below are statements supported within the supplied compilation, not independent confirmation of current vehicle specifications. Extracted text was checked against rendered table pages and footnotes; every JPG was visually inspected.

## Acceptance priorities

1. Maintain the engine-to-gearbox pairing and every variant/automatic-only condition. A feature family citation without its matrix qualifiers is insufficient.
2. Do not repeat stale FAQ values as equally current facts when this same source flags a conflicting table. Preserve conflicts for review; do not silently “repair” source records.
3. Use pictures for visible hardware and positions. File names, branding, a manual shifter, upholstery perforations or a car in a tunnel do not independently establish a different gearbox, ventilation, automatic-light behavior or ADAS.
4. Preserve normal publication, duration, language, grounding, fact-review and consent gates. This source review grants no approval to generated output before it is inspected.

## Safety and variant qualifiers

Pages 19, 31–34 and 41. Matrix legend: **S = standard in that column; dash = not offered; `*` = IVT/AT/DCT only**.

- All ten listed trims: driver/passenger plus side/curtain airbags (six total), ABS/EBD, ESC, VSM, hill-start assist, highline tyre-pressure monitoring, rear disc brakes, rear parking sensors, ISOFIX, emergency stop signal, immobilizer, burglar alarm, impact-unlock, speed-lock, three-point belts and reminders for all seats.
- Pretensioners: p32 specifically lists **driver/passenger seatbelt pretensioners** plus **driver anchor pretensioner**. Do not copy p19's loose wording into “pretensioners on all seats.”
- Rear camera with dynamic guidelines: EX(O), S(O), S(O) Knight, SX, SX Premium, King, King Knight, Lounge; not E/EX.
- Automatic headlamps: S(O), S(O) Knight, SX, SX Premium, King, King Knight, Lounge; not E/EX/EX(O).
- SmartSense collision warnings/avoidance, lane keeping/departure, attention warning, safe exit, lane following, high beam assist, departure alert and rear cross-traffic functions: King, King Knight, Lounge columns. Do not make the whole suite standard across the range.
- **Smart Cruise Control with Stop & Go: King, King Knight, Lounge, only the automatic configurations (`S*`).** A King manual citation does not support this feature.
- Surround-view and blind-spot-view monitors: SX Premium, King, King Knight, Lounge. These monitors alone do not prove that SX Premium has the whole ADAS suite.
- Dashcam: S(O), S(O) Knight, SX, SX Premium, King, King Knight, Lounge.
- SmartSense is assistance and **not a substitute for safe and attentive driving**; performance depends on conditions and fitment varies (p49). No autonomous-driving, accident-prevention guarantee or crash-rating inference.

Page25 says Bluelink is three years complimentary with 70+ features, JioSaavn one year complimentary. Bluelink needs power/network; Alexa Echo hardware is purchased separately. Avoid implying those subscription periods are a vehicle warranty.
- “Leather” references are **leatherette** per p14/p41 footnote. Variant colors/materials differ; a light-colored seat image does not prove King Knight's black/brass cabin.

## Price, warranty and unsupported answers

- Page42 body price: **starting ₹10,90,700 ex-showroom**, recorded at compilation. It is not an on-road quote, a specified-trim price, a current promise or a dealer offer. Tax, registration and insurance are additional and location-dependent. Prices/variants may change; verify with dealer before booking.
- Same page discloses structured-data price **₹10.79 lakh**, differing from body text. Preserve the discrepancy if exact price is asked; don't present the lower number as a discount or silently merge them.
- Page49: **up to seven years extended warranty applies to petrol variants only**; extended warranty and Shield of Trust packages **cost extra**. No complete base-warranty years/km contract or diesel seven-year promise is supplied. Do not equate extended maximum with free standard warranty.
- No supported numerical boot litres, ground-clearance millimetres, certified km/l, real-world mileage, crash star rating, servicing price, insurance quote, finance rate or dealer discount was found. Pages43–47 give qualitative boot/clearance answers only. Unknowns should stay unknown unless an authorized domain supplies cited live evidence later.
- Personal comfort, actual ownership cost and a person's fit cannot be guaranteed by brochure adjectives. Specific test-drive/fit advice is preferable to invented measurements.

- Turbo displacement p44 FAQ **1497 cc** conflicts with p16/p29 **1482 cm³**.
- Seven-variant FAQ and old SX(O)/SX Tech names conflict with the current ten-column matrix; p43 explicitly warns of stale FAQ values.
- Old FAQ colors include Fiery Red/Ranger Khaki/Abyss Black and six mono-tones, conflicting with p9–10's nine listed finishes. Do not infer paint availability from an asset folder/name.
- FAQ “six airbags in most variants” and “select trims have TPMS” conflict with all-ten S rows p32.
- P19 can be read as all “70+ advanced safety features” standard; matrix has many restricted features. Limit all-trim statements to specific all-S rows.
- FAQ spare-wheel text suggests premium alloys may match; p29–30 specifies **steel spares**, with different tyre sizes. Do not infer an alloy spare.
- P41 carried-over cruise footnote mentions retired SX(O)/SX Tech names; retain exact current table conditions rather than inventing columns.
- P16 has “fewer stops for charging” in combustion-engine marketing prose. Do not generate EV/charging claims for these petrol/diesel engines.
