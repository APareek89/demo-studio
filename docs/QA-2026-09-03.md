24/24 cases behave as required

Two bugs this found and fixed today: an empty (0-byte) file was accepted silently (now 400 "is empty — export it again"), and the fast-start remux wrote into a `derived/` folder that did not exist yet for videos under 60 MB (silently skipped; now created). Details in `Learning.MD`.

Notes on the accepted-but-broken cases: a corrupted JPEG/AVIF/MP4 is accepted at upload (we cannot decode 1 GB on the request path); the read stage tags what it can and reports "Image tagging unavailable… keeping the images untagged" / "ffmpeg step skipped" and continues. BMP, TIFF and AVI are rejected by design — convert to PNG/JPG/MP4.
