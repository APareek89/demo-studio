## 5. Benchmarks behind the 3-minute shape

| benchmark (public data) | what it says | how the shape applies it |
|---|---|---|
| Wistia State of Video (2023–2025) | Engagement drop-off now sits near the 60-second mark (it was ~2 min in 2016); videos ≤1 min hold ~50 % engagement, 1–5 min are roughly flat, >5 min falls sharply | The fixed opening is ≤ 60 s (≤115 words) and carries the takeaway; the whole narrated pitch is ≤ 3 min, well inside the flat zone; anything longer is Q&A the customer chooses |
| Gong Labs demo research | Losing demos contain uninterrupted pitches of up to ~106 s; winning demos open with the customer's most valuable topic, run 65:35 talk-to-listen, and the back-and-forth accelerates as the demo goes | No block exceeds ~60 words (proof) / 110 words (features) before a CONFIRM question; the runtime planner routes the customer's stated need first; check-ins and chips every block |
| Playbook (P06) | No uninterrupted explanation beyond ~60 s (~150 words) | Validator budgets per role; repair pass on breach |

Measured on the images run: opening 111 words (~45 s spoken), 496 words in total (~3.3 min at 150 wpm incl. the closing), 8 blocks each ending in a question. Sources: [Wistia — optimal video length](https://wistia.com/learn/marketing/optimal-video-length), [Wistia — State of Video statistics](https://wistia.com/blog/video-marketing-statistics), [Gong — sales demo tips backed by data](https://www.gong.io/blog/sales-demos), [Gong — talk-to-listen ratio](https://www.gong.io/blog/talk-to-listen-conversion-ratio).
