## Build the reviewed demo

1. **Sources:** store original images, video, documents and explicit owner URLs, audience, voice, languages and duration. Top-nav Studio starts a blank unsaved draft; Open Studio retains a saved demo. Duration is one to five whole minutes, three by default.
2. **Readiness:** explicit observed reasoning readiness gates Read; selected speech readiness also gates Build. Configuration alone is not readiness. Mock mode is free; real probes may cost money. Readiness overrides do not waive human approvals or grounding.
3. **Understand:** extract source-quoted facts with location, applicability and revision. Smaller page-preserving batches retain categorical features, table associations and warnings. Exact PDF table cells can verify interleaved text without fuzzy matching. Bounded embedded-picture extraction stays within this node; parent-source exclusion/revision controls derived pictures. Uploaded documents win genuine same-scope conflicts; unresolved conflicts remain held.
4. **Coach:** write `playbook.json` with reviewed category order, fundamentals before delighters, three supported USPs, objections and evidence gaps. Customer unknowns appear as gaps, never facts. Validate IDs/order and reuse only matching inputs. Picture availability does not own the story order.
5. **Plan:** map reviewed stops to approved facts, pictures and feasible 26–33-word delivery batches. Prepare enough distinct supported words for the selected duration with natural-rate headroom (at least495 at the default). Supplemental segments share section limits. Impossible supported capacity stays visible; do not manufacture coverage.
6. **Author:** write connected everyday speech from the outline, retaining source conditions and exact deeper technical detail. Existing grounding, word limits and bounded repair/completion remain. Read and revisions prepare sufficient content automatically before Script approval. Incomplete drafts remain pending; they do not spend on a ready-looking Deck. Pixel review governs literal image proof. Missing pixel decisions preserve draft choices and report them unaudited; heuristics must not replace a matching picture just for variety.
7. **Deck and FAQ:** one slide per script segment, up to two relevant pictures with their own line-bound grounded labels. Disclosed illustrations may carry factual captions without fabricated pointers. Callouts stay within three per picture/eight words, citing approved on-slide facts. FAQ answers uploaded document questions only; an empty bank auto-approves with the specified note. Guessed questions are not a Build cost.
8. **Align:** six cards remain Visuals, Facts, Script, Asked and answered, Persona & voice, and CTAs. Story order is inside Script; color approval is inside Visuals. Manual edits use existing validated routes and reset only affected approvals. Media edits retain grounded label words but retire obsolete picture attachments. Explicit missing-label creation requires approved on-slide facts, a valid line/picture and unique identity. Meaning/source scope still needs human review.
9. **Voice and Bundle:** Build records the locked voice and checks all six approvals again under the publication lock. Every published language must have the selected measured duration of distinct eligible narration. Film, Q&A, deeper-only and repeated speech do not count. Missing/corrupt audio blocks publication. A measured shortfall prepares one revised draft and returns to review without a second recording pass; old publication/audio remain intact. Successful publication pins an immutable evidence snapshot.
10. **Rehearse:** run the actual player, send feedback/uploads, or explicitly run rehearsal. Rehearsal checks customer questions first, uploaded questions next, and generates at most five only when the bank is empty. It is outside Build and does not republish or promote facts.

Automatic selective feedback routing is still pending: wording-only changes should start at Author, while new source evidence should start at its earliest affected stage. Current edits and staleness paths exist; the proposed finer-grained dependency policy is not implemented.

## Run the customer conversation

`web/player/player.js` owns playback, captions, current image/tag reveal, interruption and exact return point. `live-voice.js` owns speech transport/capture. The server runtime graph owns retrieval, reasoning, bounded tools and validation; streaming delivery stays outside replay.

- **Welcome and intake:** show the hero, choose voice/text mode, and ask one focus question. A generic show-me-around request uses the reviewed default tour without inventing a buyer preference. The optional film uses only video plus a separate Skip button.
- **Slides:** the current relevant pictures and feature tags accompany the spoken line in both default and walkthrough URLs. The gallery experiment is removed. The workbook-approved white borderless surface, template-dark text, sans-serif heading, white dock and fixed controls remain. The ordinary stage uses75% of the viewport. Two native-aspect pictures stay side by side in the fitted player; unfitted legacy previews may stack. Phone labels reserve a fixed rail above the footer; reveal changes visibility, not geometry. An intentionally unpictured slide can show its exact reviewed lines, but text cards must not replace available relevant imagery across a demo.
- **Speech ownership:** only a qualified final utterance can take a turn. Raw VAD, provisional fragments and stale finals cannot freeze the guide. Continue/Pause/Stop/Not now use local controls. Text relevance is not speaker identification and cannot reject every relevant TV sentence.
- **Questions and return:** a logical section invites questions and continues after three silent seconds. Answers and declines share the same owned window; typing, a qualified utterance or an explicit chip takes the turn. Clarifications, closing choices and explicitly opened forms retain waits. Query and resume acknowledgements differ. Healthy buffered speech refreshes its inactivity deadline.
- **Answer cache:** before graph reasoning, match eligible entries against the session's pinned snapshot/hash, then revalidate scope, dates, citations and conditions. Hits return exact saved wording/audio and increment asked counts. Skip-bank bypasses it; permanent rejection prevents serving. Only clean registry-backed validated answers enter the customer cache, with exact-text audio when completed. Tool-backed answers, failures and clarifications do not. Declines append deduplicated counted customer unknowns.
- **Live reasoning:** retrieve pinned evidence, reason, optionally use tools, then validate and create delivery. Grounding checks quantities, applicability, conditions, powertrain relationships and source attribution; citation presence alone is not semantic entailment. One bounded composition repair uses the same evidence/deadline; fallback preserves supported portions or declines.
- **Web boundary:** enabled owner URL sources permit relevant same-domain pages. Customer text, uploaded URLs and search results cannot authorize a new domain. www/apex are equivalent; other subdomains need explicit sources. PDF/image-only demos have no external runtime web. Existing public-address/redirect, model/market, two-round/four-call and twelve-second guards remain. Live evidence is attributed, turn-local and excluded from the answer cache/approved registry.
- **Follow-up:** an unanswerable question offers a button; it does not automatically open a form. Not now dismisses and resumes. Later explicit customer action can open it again. Consent stays separate from contact details; form layout cannot widen the player.
- **Sessions:** save a change after a one-second debounce, every fifteen seconds, and on visibility/exit. Preserve actually heard prefixes without prematurely marking queued speech heard. One in-flight/latest queued write and immutable retry sequence prevent stale overwrites; the server rejects older checkpoints. Ended summary ownership remains guarded, and public views mask private contact details. Current locking assumes one worker; unload beacons remain best effort.
