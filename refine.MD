## Decisions taken in this session

- D1: Open on the category's supported fundamentals in reviewed playbook order, in everyday language; delighters follow, with no opening decision frame or digits.
- D2: Coach owns story order, coverage, three USPs and gaps; Planner maps evidence, pictures and budgets; Author owns speech.
- D3: Typed segment word budgets allocate the requested length; raised role ceilings remain hard limits and measured speech remains distinct from estimates.
- D4: Up to two pictures per slide; tagged/hero fallback is labelled illustration and never counts as visual proof. This supersedes the original P1 beat-switching proposal.
- D5: Customer-supplied sites persist for the session and may be checked when evidence is missing. Product source sites join only under an explicit default-off setting. Attribution and turn scope remain mandatory.
- D6: Reviewed neutral substitutions enforce everyday runtime language; exact technical requests may retain cited terminology. Grounding never relaxes.
- D7: Narration and check-ins continue; answers listen for three seconds before resuming. Clarification, CTA and lead choices still wait.
- D8: The welcome Voice mode switch chooses capture; text remains available, mic toggles reuse the speech socket, and sessions retain mode plus each turn's actual source.
- D9: The paid trial is conditional. The brief still has placeholders for authorization and cap, so no paid Read, voice render, trial copy or output artifact was made.
