2. **Image + feature tags + matching voice remain together.** Both normal and `?presentation=walkthrough` URLs use the native slide renderer. The rejected gallery must not hide pictures/tags or replace a closing picture. Up to two relevant pictures, source-cited on-slide labels, trusted anchors only; a disclosed illustration can carry a factual caption without a fabricated pointer. Sans-serif slide headings and a fixed phone tag rail preserve readability and geometry.
3. **The 24 September workbook supersedes the older dark Marine sample.** White borderless slide, template-dark text/buttons, white conversation dock, approximately 75% viewport stage and fixed controls. Welcome/intake retains its hero image. Marine/Sage/Graphite approval stays inside Visuals. The later slide repair changes accompaniment, not the wider shell, script, sequence or voice.
4. **Narration prepares automatically.** Sources chooses 1–5 whole minutes, default three. Plan allocates feasible supported 26–33-word batches; the default preparation target is at least 495 words with natural-rate headroom. Recorded narration must reach the selected minimum in every published language. Film, Q&A, deeper-only lines, duplicates and slowdown cannot fill it. Short or failed drafting remains reviewable/pending; a measured shortfall returns a revised draft for renewed approval, preserving the previous publication.
5. **Grounding never relaxes.** Source quotes, locators, immutable fact IDs, applicability, approved evidence and pinned published snapshots govern claims. Uploaded material wins genuine same-scope conflicts. PDF picture extraction stays within Understand; pictures do not establish invisible benefits or compatibility. Shared validation now checks the observed collective engine/gearbox ambiguity, including repair and cached wording.
6. **Real customer questions drive the bank.** Build answers uploaded FAQ questions only. Clean validated registry answers can enter the snapshot-bound customer cache; reviewed edits remain exact and rejected entries never serve. Declines become counted customer unknowns, never facts; Coach exposes gaps. Rehearsal uses customer questions first and generates at most five only if the bank is empty.
7. **Runtime web access is owner-domain-only.** Enabled top-level owner URL sources grant permission to relevant pages on the same domain. Customer text, PDF URLs and search results cannot grant new domains; PDF/image-only demos have no external runtime web access. www/apex are equivalent; other subdomains require their own source. This supersedes the earlier public-web permission.
8. **Speech and conversation stay owned by the player.** Qualified final utterances can interrupt; raw onset, partial recognition and fragments cannot take a turn. Continue/Pause/Stop/Not now are local controls. Generic tour intake does not invent preferences. Logical sections and answers allow three silent seconds before continuing; clarifications, closing and explicitly opened forms retain their waits. Healthy stream progress refreshes its timeout.
9. **Save during the visit.** Incremental session checkpoints preserve actually heard speech with ordered, retryable writes. Follow-up forms require a customer action, honor dismissal, and remain inside the viewport. Contact consent and public/private separation stand.
10. **One locked voice, audio-led visuals, no-build frontend.** Provider configuration is not readiness. Text defaults are Gemini → Claude → Runware; voice identity remains locked across stored/streamed speech. No browser-voice substitution for a locked demo. No keys in git or browser output.
