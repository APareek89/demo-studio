4. **Narration prepares automatically.** Sources chooses 1–5 whole minutes, default three. Plan allocates feasible supported 26–33-word batches; the default preparation target is at least 495 words with natural-rate headroom. Recorded narration must reach the selected minimum in every published language. Film, Q&A, deeper-only lines, duplicates and slowdown cannot fill it. Short or failed drafting remains reviewable/pending; a measured shortfall returns a revised draft for renewed approval, preserving the previous publication.
5. **Grounding never relaxes.** Source quotes, locators, immutable fact IDs, applicability, approved evidence and pinned published snapshots govern claims. Uploaded material wins genuine same-scope conflicts. PDF picture extraction stays within Understand; pictures do not establish invisible benefits or compatibility. Shared validation now checks the observed collective engine/gearbox ambiguity, including repair and cached wording.

Clean validated registry answers can enter the snapshot-bound customer cache; reviewed edits remain exact and rejected entries never serve. Declines become counted customer unknowns, never facts; Coach exposes gaps. Rehearsal uses customer questions first and generates at most five only if the bank is empty.
7. **Runtime web access is owner-domain-only.** Enabled top-level owner URL sources grant permission to relevant pages on the same domain. Customer text, PDF URLs and search results cannot grant new domains; PDF/image-only demos have no external runtime web access. www/apex are equivalent; other subdomains require their own source. This supersedes the earlier public-web permission.
8. **Speech and conversation stay owned by the player.** Sustained local speech-like frames or qualified partials can reversibly pause output; only a qualified final takes a semantic turn. Raw energy alone and rejected fragments cannot create a turn. A discarded hold resumes the same clip, with bounded silence/deadline recovery. Continue/Pause/Stop/Not now are local controls. Generic tour intake does not invent preferences. Logical sections and answers allow three silent seconds before continuing; clarifications, closing and explicitly opened forms retain their waits. Healthy stream progress refreshes its timeout.
9. **Save during the visit.** Incremental session checkpoints preserve actually heard speech with ordered, retryable writes. Follow-up forms require a customer action, honor dismissal, and remain inside the viewport. Contact consent and public/private separation stand.
10. **One locked voice, audio-led visuals, no-build frontend.** Provider configuration is not readiness.
